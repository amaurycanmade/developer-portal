News, Notices & Fraud Alerts

Official notices from Karen Johnson, Register of Deeds.

⚠️ Warning: Deed Copy Solicitation Scam

Do not pay $80.00 to $100.00 for a copy of your deed. Official certified copies are available directly from the Register of Deeds office for only $0.50 to $1.00 per page under T.C.A. §10-7-515.
