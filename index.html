About The Hub

Common questions regarding licensing, research submission, and collaboration.

All assets published in the public catalog are released under the MIT or Creative Commons (CC BY 4.0) licenses, depending on the contributor. This allows for broad use in both personal and commercial game development projects without restrictive limitations. Always verify the specific license attached to individual downloads.
We welcome partnerships with universities and research labs. Institutional partners receive access to aggregate, anonymized developer usage data to aid in studies on human-computer interaction, software engineering, and digital arts. Please contact our stakeholder relations team to initiate a partnership.
Registered developers can propose assets through the portal dashboard. Submissions undergo a peer-review process by our internal engineering team to ensure code safety, performance metrics, and appropriate documentation before being accepted into the public catalog.
